# Fix: launch freezing 10 000 recipients over the 2 s budget under full-suite load

## Symptom

The 10 000-recipient launch measured 0.54 s alone and 3.1 s under the full parallel suite against
the spec §6 budget of 2 s.

## Root cause

Measured with the timing laps of one launch (alone): locks 2 ms, audience evaluation 30 ms, the
recipient insert ~520 ms, the rest ~40 ms. `EXPLAIN (ANALYZE)` of the one `unnest` insert: 81 ms for
the rows, then 117 + 120 + 130 ms in the three foreign-key checks the data model requires (workspace,
broadcast, contact), 450 ms in all. The same statement took 3.2–4.0 s while other suites ran (load
average 10–19). The cost is the integrity checks scaling with machine load, not an algorithm: there
is no N+1 and no row-by-row work.

Two real defects sat next to it and are fixed (review A3, Q4): the confirmation's breakdown and the
reachable set came from two statements (a block landing between them made the count and the frozen
rows differ), and every contacts and bots read of the launch ran on a second pool connection while
the launch transaction held its locks. Both now go through one `contacts.launchAudience` statement
inside the launch transaction (one audience query instead of two).

## The pinning test

- Unit `broadcasts/app/launch.test.ts` › "AC-06: inside its transaction the launch reads contacts
  and bots on that transaction, never on a second connection". RED: `expected [ 'launchReadiness',
  'audienceBreakdown', 'reachableContacts', 'resolveTags' ] to deeply equal []`.
- Unit › "AC-06: the confirmation counts exactly the set it fingerprints, even when a block lands
  while it reads". RED: the fingerprint covered 2 contacts while the count said 3.
- Integration `contacts/infra/audience-query.int.test.ts` › launchAudience (in a transaction, two
  workspaces).

## Spec patch

None — the 2 s budget is unchanged. ADR-0006 and SAD §5 name `launchAudience` in the launch
transaction; test-plan records the measurement and the existing best-of-three policy for
server-time budgets on a shared machine.

## Follow-ups

- The plain `broadcast_recipients.workspace_id` foreign key is implied by the composite broadcast
  key; dropping it would save about a quarter of the insert. A schema change (expand → contract),
  not done in a review fix.
