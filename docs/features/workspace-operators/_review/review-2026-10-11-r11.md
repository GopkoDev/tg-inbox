## Accepted residual

The reviewer listed two raw spellings as "residual, not raised". The user accepted them as residual risk:

- **Double-encoded separators** such as `/invite%255C<t>` and `/invite%252F<t>` are logged unchanged. Both routers see a single segment (`invite%5Ctok`), so neither resolves them to a link route.
- **A semicolon right after the prefix**, as in `/invite;p/<t>`, is logged unchanged. This was already the case before T58. react-router sees the segment `invite;p` and does not match it.

The masking policy (rounds 6–11) is:

- mask every form that the SPA router or Fastify resolves to a link route;
- mask the encoded twins of every separator the mask already handles (`%2F`, `%5C`) and dot segments;
- do not chase further raw spellings that open no route.

This is recorded in `contracts/api-sync-report.md`.
