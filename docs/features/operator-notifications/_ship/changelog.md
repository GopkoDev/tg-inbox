# Changelog — operator-notifications

## operator-notifications — operators see what needs them: an attention count in the tab and the navigation, desktop notifications, and a Telegram ping from the business bot when they are away

**What:** The shared inbox now reaches operators when a customer is waiting. Operators and business owners of a workspace (same rights for both) get three layers of attention.

- **Attention count.** The browser tab title starts with "(N)" and the Inbox item in the side navigation (and the phone header) shows N: the number of open conversations that are the member's or unassigned and have unread customer messages for them. It changes live as messages arrive, are read, and as conversations are assigned, closed or reopened. At 0 nothing is shown. Each tab counts its own workspace.
- **Desktop notifications.** In Settings — Notifications a member can turn on desktop notifications for this browser (the permission is requested only on their click). A notification is held back only while a tab of that workspace in this browser is in focus; one notification replaces the previous one, and a click opens the most recently focused tab at the conversation. A notifications-only service worker, `/notifications-sw.js`, shows them.
- **Telegram pings.** A member links their own Telegram account to the workspace's bot with a single-use connect link (10 minutes, at most 5 per 10 minutes). When a customer has waited 5 minutes and the member is away from the web and has not read the message, the bot sends them a ping with no message text and no contact name, linking to the right inbox lens. A conversation stays due while the wait lasts, so a member who becomes away later is pinged then. At most one ping per conversation per waiting period and one per member per 10 minutes; conversations that become due in between are combined into the next ping. If the assignee cannot be pinged (no link, or not reachable), or the conversation is unassigned, the rest of the team is pinged, the team at 30 minutes when the assignee can be reached.
- **Honest states.** The panel shows Connected, Not connected, "Pings paused — the bot is not working" and "Not reachable — you blocked the bot in Telegram". A business owner sees in Team which members have Telegram pings on or off, never the Telegram account itself.
- **The notification chat is not a customer.** Once linked, every private message from that person to the bot (text, media, edits, any start link) is consumed, not captured as a customer conversation; the bot answers at most once per hour that the chat only sends notifications. Disconnecting makes them an ordinary end customer again.
- **Lifecycle.** Deactivating a member removes their link; connecting a different bot to the workspace removes every link made with the old one; rotating the token or disconnecting the bot keeps links (pings pause).

**Why:** An inbox nobody is looking at loses customers; the idea brief names missed messages as the main risk, and roadmap step 6 closes it without a second channel for replies ([spec](../spec.md) §1–§2; it also answers bot-connection §8: a broken bot cannot alert through Telegram, so the inbox notice stays the channel). Key decisions:

- [ADR-0001](../adr/0001-add-a-notifications-module-that-owns-telegram-links-presence-and-pings-and-is-reached-through-ports.md): a `notifications` module owns links, presence and pings and is reached through ports.
- [ADR-0002](../adr/0002-track-presence-as-per-tab-leases-in-postgres-refreshed-by-the-workspace-live-socket.md): presence is per-tab leases in Postgres, refreshed by the workspace live socket.
- [ADR-0003](../adr/0003-intercept-connect-link-starts-and-linked-members-messages-in-bots-before-capture-through-a-port.md): `bots` intercepts connect-link starts and linked members' messages before capture, through a port.
- [ADR-0004](../adr/0004-issue-connect-links-as-random-single-use-tokens-stored-as-hashes-and-redeemed-through-the-start-payload.md): connect links are random single-use tokens stored as hashes, redeemed through the start payload.
- [ADR-0005](../adr/0005-decide-pings-in-a-per-workspace-sweep-every-20-s-with-a-per-period-ledger-written-before-the-send.md): pings are decided in a per-workspace sweep every 20 s, with a per-period ledger written before the send.
- [ADR-0006](../adr/0006-pace-pings-below-a-per-bot-share-of-the-telegram-limit-and-pause-them-on-any-rate-limit-answer.md): pings are paced below a per-bot share of the Telegram limit and pause on any rate-limit answer; replies go first.
- [ADR-0007](../adr/0007-record-the-waiting-period-start-on-the-conversation-in-the-writing-transactions.md): the waiting-period start is recorded on the conversation in the writing transactions.
- [ADR-0008](../adr/0008-serve-the-attention-count-from-a-conversations-query-refetched-on-change-signals.md): the attention count is a `conversations` query refetched on change signals.
- [ADR-0009](../adr/0009-show-desktop-notifications-through-a-notifications-only-service-worker-that-checks-focus-and-picks-the-tab.md): desktop notifications go through a notifications-only service worker that checks focus and picks the tab.
- [ADR-0010](../adr/0010-signal-telegram-link-changes-on-an-account-scoped-frame-delivered-only-to-that-members-sockets.md): Telegram link changes are signalled on an account-scoped frame delivered only to that member's sockets.

**How to use:** In the panel open *Settings — Notifications*: connect Telegram (the dialog shows the link with Copy and Open in Telegram, waits for Start and turns to Connected live), disconnect, and turn on desktop notifications for this browser. In Telegram, opening the link starts the bot with a `tgi_…` payload; the bot confirms the link in the chat. The HTTP surface is in [openapi.yaml](../contracts/openapi.yaml):

- `GET /api/workspaces/:workspaceId/notifications` — the caller's settings (Telegram state, bot readiness).
- `POST /api/workspaces/:workspaceId/notifications/telegram/connect-link` — issue a connect link (201); a newer link replaces the unused one.
- `POST /api/workspaces/:workspaceId/notifications/telegram/disconnect` — remove the caller's own link.
- `GET /api/workspaces/:workspaceId/inbox/attention` — the caller's attention count.
- Refusals: three new error codes (declared in `packages/shared/src/errors.ts`), including the connect-link limit (429 with the retry time).
- Live: the `GET /live` socket becomes one per-tab workspace connection that carries presence frames (client to server) and the account-scoped `telegram_link.changed` frame, see [events.md](../contracts/events.md). `GET …/team` memberships carry `telegram_pings`.

**Operational notes:**

- **Migrations:** eight reversible migrations, `1791681109998_add_bots_send_paused_until` … `1791681110005_add_conversations_waiting_index` (the bot send pause, `telegram_links`, `connect_links`, `tab_presences`, `pings` + `ping_items`, the conversation waiting-period columns, their backfill and index). Run `pnpm db:migrate` on deploy before the new API starts. **Dev databases that applied `1791681110001` before commit `485f33f` must `pnpm db:rollback` and migrate again:** the `tab_presences` check constraint was changed in place.
- **Rollback:** revert the deploy, then `pnpm db:rollback` eight times. This drops every Telegram link, ping record and presence lease (conversations, messages and replies stay; members must link Telegram again).
- **Jobs (pg-boss):** `notifications.send-pings` (policy `stately`, `singletonKey` = workspace id, retry 0, expire 60 s — a per-workspace sweep chain every 20 s), `notifications.schedule-pings` (cron every minute, restarts chains), `notifications.send-bot-message` (retry 2 with back-off, expire 30 s) and its dead-letter queue `notifications.send-bot-message-dlq`. All are created and registered by `buildApp()`.
- **Tenancy:** this feature adds the sixth controlled exception — *workspaces with a live Telegram link* in `notifications` (workspace ids only, used only by the `schedule-pings` backstop; ADR-0005), recorded in `CLAUDE.md` and the architecture map.
- **Service worker:** `apps/web/public/notifications-sw.js` is served as `/notifications-sw.js` with `text/javascript` and `no-cache` (it is never cached, so updates reach browsers); it only shows notifications, no fetch caching.
- **Config:** no new keys (`apps/api/src/platform/config.ts` and `.env.example` are unchanged on this branch). **Feature flag:** none. Pings need a working bot and `TELEGRAM_WEBHOOK_BASE_URL` as before.
- **Run-book integrity queries:** the eight queries in `test-plan.md` §6 (pings to inactive members, duplicate ledger items, pings less than 10 minutes apart, contacts equal to a linked member, links of another bot, live links of inactive members, member-removed links still holding a Telegram id after 1 h, waiting conversations without a period) — all expect 0 rows; implemented as `notificationIntegrity.*` in `apps/api/src/e2e/helpers.ts`.
- **Pending security review (F-3):** the Telegram user id is kept in `notifications.send-bot-message` job payloads for the `link_expired` / `cannot_connect` answers (and in their dead-letter queue), and in the redeem debug log line; a member-removed link's id is cleared by its `notifications_off` job. Accepted pending the security review that spec §6.1 requires.
- **Not yet done:** real-bot verification (link flow, pings, answers) still waits for a public HTTPS host, and real OS desktop notifications need a headed browser; both are a manual pre-release check. No QR code in v1 (no new dependency).

**Acceptance criteria delivered:** AC-01 – AC-13 — attention count (AC-01), desktop notifications and their blocked-permission state (AC-02, AC-03), connect link, its refusals and the replace rule (AC-04, AC-05, AC-06), the 5-minute ping (AC-07), once per period, 10-minute spacing and combining (AC-08), the unreachable assignee and the team ping (AC-09), pausing, blocked and lifecycle rules (AC-10), the linked chat is not captured (AC-11), access control (AC-12), the connect-link limit (AC-13).
