# Domain Context — tg-inbox

## Glossary

- Account — a person's single sign-in identity on the platform (email + password), shared across every workspace they belong to; its email is compared ignoring letter case and surrounding spaces. NOT Operator (an operator is the role an account holds inside one workspace).
- Active pilot workspace — a pilot workspace where, within the last 30 days, a customer wrote to its business bot and a member replied; the denominator of the pilot adoption metrics. NOT an active Membership (that is a person's state in a workspace) and NOT a workspace with a connected bot (a connected bot can sit idle).
- Activity entry — a line in a conversation's thread recording a change to its assignee or status — who made it and when, or the automatic cause (the customer wrote, a reply was not delivered, the assignee was deactivated); it sits at the time it happened and is never sent to the end customer. NOT Reply and NOT Message.
- Arrival watermark — the per-member, per-conversation point in arrival order up to which that member has read the conversation; it only moves forward and never past the newest arrival. NOT a read receipt to the end customer (Telegram never learns of it) and NOT shared by the team (each member has their own).
- Assignee — the one active member of a workspace a conversation is assigned to, who is expected to handle it; a conversation has at most one assignee or is unassigned, and any member can assign, reassign or unassign it. NOT Author (the member who wrote a reply) and NOT a lock (teammates can still read and reply).
- Attention count — the number of open conversations of a workspace that are assigned to a member or unassigned and hold customer messages unread by that member; shown in that workspace's browser tab title and next to Inbox in the navigation. NOT the per-row unread count (that counts messages in one conversation) and NOT Waiting for a reply.
- Away from the web — a member's state for one workspace when none of their browser tabs, on any device, has that workspace's panel open with a live connection working right now and on screen at some moment within the last 10 minutes (a closed tab, sleeping computer or lost connection stops counting within 60 s; a connected tab hidden behind another tab or minimised, 10 minutes after it left the screen); only an away member can be pinged. NOT a deactivated membership (an away member is still active) and NOT an availability status a member sets.
- Business bot — the workspace's own Telegram bot, created by the business in BotFather and connected to the workspace by a business owner pasting its token; end customers write to it and it belongs to at most one workspace. NOT a platform-wide bot (tg-inbox has no shared bot; every workspace brings its own).
- Business owner — a workspace member with the owner role: manages the workspace's team (invites, deactivates and reactivates operators and other business owners) and can do everything an operator can. NOT Platform admin (an owner manages only their own workspace).
- Change signal — a small live notice («this conversation, item, bot or contact's tags changed») pushed to the open panel tabs of one workspace (inbox, contact profile), carrying ids only; the browser then re-reads what changed. NOT the change itself (it carries no text or content) and NOT a notification to the end customer.
- Client reply key — the id the browser gives a reply before sending it, so a repeated submit of the same reply finds it instead of creating a second one. NOT the reply's own id and NOT a Telegram message id.
- Close snapshot — what a member's close was shown: the conversation's customer message counter plus the latest reply's id and state; the close is re-checked under the conversation row lock against it, and asks again when it no longer matches. NOT the state version (that guards the whole change, the snapshot only the close reasons).
- Connect link — the single-use link, valid for 10 minutes and bound to one membership, that a member opens in Telegram to create their Telegram link; starting the bot through it is never captured as a customer message. NOT an Invitation (it joins nobody to a workspace) and NOT a business's own start link (those are captured as before).
- Contact — an end customer as known to one workspace, created automatically from their Telegram profile (name, username, language) the first time they write to the workspace's business bot and refreshed on later messages, and grouped by the team with the workspace's tags; the same person writing to two workspaces' bots is two separate contacts. NOT Account (a contact never signs in) and NOT End customer in general (a contact is that person's record inside one workspace).
- Conversation — the one continuous thread between a workspace and one contact, holding every message that contact sent to the business bot (and, from the inbox step on, the operators' replies). NOT Message (a conversation holds many messages) and NOT a support ticket (it is not opened per issue; it is closed and reopened, never replaced).
- Conversation status — whether a conversation still needs the team (open) or is done (closed); a closed conversation leaves the default working list and opens again when its end customer writes a new message or one of its replies ends not delivered or not confirmed. NOT Delivery state (that belongs to a reply) and NOT Waiting for a reply (a derived marker, not a member's decision).
- Customer message counter — a per-conversation count raised by capture, under the conversation row lock, for every newly captured customer message; it orders arrivals by commit. NOT a message id and NOT the unread count (that is per member).
- Delivery state — what a reply shows about its way to Telegram: sending, sent, not delivered (with its reason) or not confirmed. NOT read status (tg-inbox never knows whether the end customer read a reply).
- Desktop notification — a system notification the web panel shows on a member's computer, in a browser where they turned it on and that has a tab of that workspace open, for a new customer message in a conversation that is theirs or unassigned while no tab of that workspace in that browser is in focus; it names the contact and workspace, never the message text. NOT a Ping (that goes through Telegram when the member is away).
- Drain — the per-conversation delivery job that sends that conversation's pending replies one at a time, oldest first. NOT a broadcast and NOT a retry of an unconfirmed reply (those are resent only by a member).
- Due set — the conversations a member should be pinged about right now, worked out by the ping sweep from the waiting periods, reads, presence, the ledger and the member's last ping; it is recomputed for each member inside their own transaction before a ping is recorded. NOT the Ping (the message sent) and NOT a stored queue (nothing holds it between sweeps).
- End customer — a person who writes to a workspace's bot in Telegram and never uses the web panel. NOT Operator (operators answer end customers from the web).
- First wrote / last wrote — the Telegram send times of a contact's oldest and newest customer messages, shown on the Contacts list and the profile and ordering the list newest first; replies and edits never move them, and a contact that never wrote shows «Never». NOT the conversation's last activity (a reply or a status change moves that) and NOT when the contact record was created or refreshed.
- In focus — a panel tab that is the selected tab of the browser window the member is working in; only an in-focus tab holds desktop notifications back. NOT On screen (a visible but unfocused tab is on screen, not in focus).
- Inbound interceptor — the hook `bots` calls for every private update before it is captured, so that a chat with the business bot that is not a customer message (starting through a connect link, or a member's own chat) is answered by `notifications` and never becomes a contact or conversation. NOT a webhook (it runs after Telegram's update was accepted) and NOT a filter on customers.
- Inbox — the web-panel screen where a workspace's operators and business owners see all of the workspace's conversations, read them and reply; it is shared by the whole team, not one per operator. NOT Conversation (the inbox lists many conversations) and NOT the business bot's own chat in Telegram (operators never work there).
- Invitation — a single-use, expiring link for one email address and one role, through which that person joins the workspace; created by a business owner, or by a platform admin for a workspace's first business owner. NOT Membership (an invitation only offers a membership until it is accepted).
- Lens — one of the inbox list's two filters a member chooses: status (open or closed) and assignee (all, mine or unassigned); a lens only narrows what is shown. NOT a saved search and NOT a per-member queue.
- Membership — the link between an account and a workspace, carrying a role (owner / operator) and a state (active / deactivated). NOT Account (deactivating a membership leaves the account and its other memberships untouched).
- Message — one thing an end customer sent to the business bot in a private chat (text, or another kind such as a photo, voice or sticker, with its caption), captured into their conversation; an edit replaces its captured text. NOT Conversation.
- Name key — a tag's tidied name with letter case folded and the invisible joiners left out (so «VIP», «vip», « VIP » and «VIP» with a hidden joiner inside are one key); the value that is unique per workspace and decides whether a typed name is an existing tag. NOT the shown name (the tag keeps the case it was given) and NOT the tag's id (a rename changes the key, never the id).
- Not confirmed — the delivery state of a reply whose send to Telegram got no answer, so it may or may not have reached the end customer; it is never resent without a member confirming the risk of a duplicate. NOT Not delivered (Telegram answered and refused or did not accept it).
- On screen — a panel tab that is the selected tab of a browser window that is not minimised, on an awake computer; used for presence (Away from the web). NOT In focus (on screen does not need the member to be working in that window).
- Operator — a workspace member who answers end customers from the shared web inbox. NOT End customer; NOT Business owner (an operator does not manage the team).
- Ping — a short private message the business bot sends to a member's Telegram link when customers have waited for a reply while the member is away from the web — after 5 minutes for the member's own or unassigned conversations (or a teammate's whose assignee is away with no link or an unreachable one), after 30 minutes for a teammate's; at most one per member per workspace per 10 minutes, combining the conversations newly due; it names the workspace, how many wait and the longest wait, links to the panel, and never carries customer text or a contact name. NOT a Reply (it never reaches an end customer) and NOT a Change signal (that goes to open panel tabs).
- Ping ledger — the record of which member was pinged about which conversation in which waiting period, written before the send and never rewritten; it is what makes a ping go out at most once per waiting period. NOT the Ping itself (one ping holds many ledger rows) and NOT a delivery log for replies.
- Platform admin — a member of the tg-inbox team who creates workspaces by hand together with the invitation for their first business owner, and issues password-reset links on request (there is no self-serve signup). NOT Business owner (a platform admin acts across workspaces, outside the web panel).
- Position time — where an item sits in a conversation's thread: a message by its Telegram send time (second precision), a reply by when a member (re)sent it, an activity entry by when the change happened; edits never move an item. NOT arrival time (a late redelivery arrives late but sits at its send time).
- Presence lease — a per-tab record, valid for 50 seconds and extended by the tab's live connection, with whether the tab is on screen; a member with no unexpired on-screen lease for a workspace in the last 10 minutes is away from the web. NOT a session (it says nothing about being signed in) and NOT a login time.
- Reply — a text an operator or business owner writes in a conversation in the inbox, which the business bot sends to that conversation's end customer in Telegram; the team sees which member wrote it, the end customer sees only the bot. NOT Message (a message comes from the end customer) and NOT a broadcast (a reply goes to one end customer, inside their conversation).
- Send pause — the time stored on the business bot until which Telegram asked us to stop sending after a rate-limit answer; pings and the bot's answers wait for it and replies to customers never do. NOT the bot's health state (a paused bot still works) and NOT a per-member limit.
- State version — the per-conversation integer raised by every change of assignee or status, by a member or automatically; a member's change carries the version they saw, and a mismatch asks them first. NOT an optimistic lock on the whole row (messages and replies do not raise it).
- Tag — a short name (1–32 characters as a person sees them, stored with its ends trimmed and inner spaces collapsed to one, unique in the workspace ignoring letter case and spacing; it stays the same tag across renames) that members put on a workspace's contacts to group them; any member adds, removes and creates tags on contacts, only a business owner renames, merges or deletes a tag, each tag on a contact records who added it and when, and the contact's tag history keeps every addition, removal, merge and delete with the member and time, under the name the tag had then; a workspace has at most 100 tags and a contact at most 20. NOT an Activity entry (tags belong to the contact, not to a conversation) and NOT a Lens (a lens narrows the inbox list; tags filter the Contacts list).
- Tag filter — the Contacts list's choice of tags with a match mode — any of the chosen tags, all of them, or no tags — together with the number of contacts that match; the size of a future broadcast segment. NOT a saved segment (nothing is stored) and NOT a Lens.
- Tag history — the per-contact, append-only record of every tag added, removed, merged into another tag or removed by a delete, with the member, the time, where it was done (inbox, profile or settings) and the tag's name at that moment; it is never rewritten, so a later rename or delete leaves old entries as they were. NOT an Activity entry (that belongs to a conversation's thread) and NOT the list of tags a contact carries now.
- Tag-list order — the one order tags are shown in everywhere (the picker, the filter, the profile, list rows and Settings → Tags): dictionary order of their names ignoring letter case, with the name key as the tie-break. NOT the order tags were created or added to a contact, and NOT the order of the tag history (that is newest first).
- Telegram link — the opt-in connection between one membership and the member's own personal Telegram account through that workspace's business bot, through which the bot pings them; made and removed only by the member, removed by deactivation or when the workspace connects a different bot. Nothing a linked account sends to that bot is captured, including starts through a business's own campaign link. NOT a Contact (a linked member is not an end customer of that workspace) and NOT a way to sign in or to reply from Telegram.
- Tidied name — a tag name with invisible zero-width spaces removed (the joiners that hold emoji and some scripts together are kept, except next to a space), every run of spaces collapsed to one and its ends trimmed; what is stored and shown, and what the 1–32 character rule is measured on (an emoji counts as one). NOT the Name key (the tidied name keeps its letter case) and NOT the text as typed.
- Unread — the customer messages that arrived in tg-inbox after a member's arrival watermark and after their membership became active; counted per member, by arrival. NOT unanswered (see Waiting for a reply) and never counts replies.
- Waiting for a reply — a conversation whose end customer wrote after the last reply marked sent; the one shared marker before conversation statuses exist. NOT Unread (waiting is the same for the whole team).
- Waiting period — the span from the customer message that makes a conversation waiting for a reply (or from a reopen while its customer is still waiting) until a reply is marked sent or the conversation is closed; its ping clocks run from its start and a reassignment does not restart them; each member is pinged at most once per conversation per waiting period. NOT Unread (a member can read a message and the customer still waits).
- Workspace — one business on the platform and the boundary of all its data (bot, conversations, contacts, team). NOT Account (one account can belong to several workspaces).

## Invariants

- A workspace's data can never be seen or changed by anyone who is not an active member of that workspace.
- Once its first business owner invitation is accepted, a workspace always has at least one active business owner.
- A business bot is connected to at most one workspace, and a workspace has at most one business bot.
- A workspace has at most one contact and one conversation per end customer.
- A contact carries only tags of its own workspace, and a workspace never holds two tags with the same name (ignoring letter case and spacing).
- A conversation has at most one assignee, and an assignee is always an active member of the conversation's workspace.
- A member has at most one Telegram link per workspace, a Telegram account is linked to at most one member of a workspace, and only an active member has a Telegram link.
